Direction: pale-blue daytime hero, glass architecture and warm interiors, sunset explanatory section, deep navy evidence/footer. Bold close-set headings, restrained blue actions, subtle cyan evidence accents. Static building artwork; no heavy animation framework or WebGL.

Shared components implemented: Brand, Button, ThemeSelector, Header, Modal, EvidencePreview. For Builder, Buyer, Seller and the complete theme selector share control dimensions. Desktop: 132×42 px; tablet: 112×42 px; mobile: width clamp(84px,26.7vw,104px), height 44 px. Navigation controls share weight, radius, hover and focus treatment. Mobile navigation places all three role buttons on one row, with How it works on its own row.

Themes: Day / Auto / Night with persisted preference. Auto follows operating-system preference. Honor reduced motion and visible keyboard focus. All foreground content remains actual selectable HTML.

Implemented tokens: primary blue #0064f6, daylight canvas #c7e7fc, sunset canvas #ffdbab, night canvas #04172e, primary ink #07192e, muted daylight #344e65. Night text #e6f3ff, muted #b9cee0. Inter is bundled locally at 400/500/600/700 weights. Hero heading scales with viewport; shared controls have 12–15 px radii, evidence panel 19 px and dialogs 22 px.

Responsive strategy: architecture remains scenic on desktop; tablet heading and scene sizing adapt to keep text readable; mobile stacks copy above buildings and evidence, maintaining original section order. No horizontal page overflow at 1440, 820, 390 or 320 px. Reduced motion disables transitions; no animation framework installed.

Accessibility: semantic headings/landmarks, skip link, labeled native radio group, visible focus, native modal dialog plus explicit Tab/Shift+Tab wrap and Escape dismissal, return focus to trigger, icon names where meaningful. Image backgrounds are decorative with empty alt text; all business information is in HTML.

Buyer shell: 246px navy sidebar at large desktop, 54px utility header, pale #f1f8fe canvas, translucent white cards with #d9e7f0 borders, 19–20px radii, blue #005df5 actions. Slate #566276 secondary text. Dark mode uses #0b1b2e canvas, #12283f cards and #b6c6db secondary text. Sidebar narrows at 1350px and becomes an accessible icon rail at 1100px; below 761px it becomes a top navigation. Workspace theme selector is 196×38px desktop and 144×34px mobile; landing controls retain their own approved dimensions.

Typography and icons remain bundled locally. Buyer CSS is page-scoped. Warm yellow buyer-reported concern and blue READY badge do not imply success or payment. Static CSS trophy is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
